Chopped Basalt Fibre
A high-performance reinforcing fibre that combines strength, thermal stability and chemical resistance — a cost-effective alternative to steel, glass and carbon fibre. With more than 70 million monofilaments per kilogram, basalt fibre binds concrete in three dimensions for dramatically improved strength and abrasion resistance.

Why Basalt Fibre
Benefits in Concrete
Mixed into concrete, asphalt, resin or plastic, basalt fibre reinforces from within — lifting strength and durability while cutting long-term maintenance.
Key Benefits
Impact & Splitting Resistance A three-dimensional fibre network resists dynamic loads, splitting and impact.
Stronger Bond Improves binding strength and load transfer through the concrete matrix.
Abrasion & Wear Resistance Stands up to heavy traffic — industrial floors, highways and hard-wearing surfaces.
Fire & Thermal Stability Non-combustible volcanic rock with a melting point near 1,450 °C.
Chemical, Alkali & Frost Resistance Performs in aggressive, marine and freeze–thaw environments.
Less Cracking, No Delamination Restrains shrinkage cracks during curing and keeps the mix homogeneous.

Properties & Specification
Basalt fibre is stronger than steel and polypropylene fibre, and stretches less than glass fibre — so loaded concrete cracks and shrinks less, and stays intact for longer.

Engineered Coating
A water-based PVC coating (carboxylate styrene-butadiene latex) improves chemical bonding with cement, adhesion to aggregates, mechanical stability and water resistance.

Mixing Tip
Mix the fibres dry with sand, stone and cement, then add water. Extend mixing by 30–45 seconds for even dispersion. Beyond the saturation point, extra fibre stops adding strength.
